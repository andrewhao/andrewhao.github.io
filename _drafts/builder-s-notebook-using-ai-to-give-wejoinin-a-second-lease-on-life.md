<h2 class=“intro”>In which we talk about how I’m wielding AI as a tool to give new life to a long-lived side project</h2>

Wejoinin is a hosted service that helps organizations organize signups for their events. My friend Hsiu-Fan and I have been maintaining Wejoinin as a labor of love since 2007 - bright-eyed, bushy-tailed college students in the original Web 2.0 era.

However, over the years life took over. We got jobs and started careers - and eventually families. We kept the site running on a shoestring budget and told ourselves that as long as we could, we’d keep the service running as long as we had users.

And to our surprise, we had some pretty loyal users year over year. Site traffic stayed consistent and even boomed during the COVID pandemic. Users came to see our service as a trusted place they could use to throw together a quick sheet and get folks organized.

I even wrote a quick medium post on our ten-year anniversary, titled [“Wejoinin at Ten: Our story thus far”](https://medium.com/wejoinin/wejoinin-at-ten-34efeaff6e03). Though the post was very upbeat about the new happenings on our service, there was one problem - we never had enough time to see all our ambitions through.
